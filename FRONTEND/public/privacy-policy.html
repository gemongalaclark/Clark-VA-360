Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Overview

Clark VA 360 ("we", "our", "the Service") operates the Google Calendar Auto Scheduler at clark-va-360.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect the following through Google OAuth:

  • Your Google account name and email address
  • Your Google profile picture (avatar)
  • A temporary OAuth access token used solely to create calendar events on your behalf

We store your name, email, avatar URL, and sign-in timestamp in our secure Supabase database to identify your account and display your profile within the app.

We do not store your Google OAuth access token in our database. It is held only in your browser session and is discarded when you sign out.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and display your name and profile picture inside the app
  • To create Google Calendar events that you explicitly review and approve
  • To maintain a session history of schedule scans you have performed (stored locally in your browser)

We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to any third party.

4. Google Calendar Access

This app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events scope, which allows us to create events in your Google Calendar only. We do not read, modify, or delete existing events in your calendar. Events are only created after you review and confirm the schedule preview.

You may revoke this access at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing Clark VA 360 / cdlvlgtemdlhbvhihvle.supabase.co.

5. Data Storage & Security

User profile data (name, email, avatar) is stored in Supabase, a GDPR-compliant cloud database provider, with row-level security policies that ensure only your own data is accessible to you. Schedule scan history is stored in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to our servers.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth & Calendar API – for authentication and calendar event creation
  • Google Gemini AI – for extracting schedule data from uploaded files. File content is sent to Google's Gemini API and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • Supabase – for secure user data storage
  • Vercel – for hosting the web application

7. Data Retention

Your profile data (name, email, avatar) is retained in our database while you have an account. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us at the address below and we will remove your record within 7 days.

8. Children's Privacy

This Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
